Pawbound v4.4 Beta — Country guide answer visibility update

Open the Pawbound site, go to Travel Rules > Approved Pet Entry Points, select destination, departure country and animal, and tap Confirm & Show My Answer.
A green success panel should appear immediately below the button. The detailed country guide appears below that panel and the page should automatically scroll to it.
If you downloaded an HTML file and see content://media/external at the top of your browser, open the deployed HTTPS Netlify site instead. A downloaded HTML file may block app storage and updates.
This is a location/interaction fix only, not a new verification of country rules. Countries without verified government requirements remain clearly labeled.
