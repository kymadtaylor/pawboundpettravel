Pawbound privacy information · Beta

Current beta: Pet records, travel plans, packing lists, and uploaded photos are generally stored in your browser's local storage on your device. A free email/password account is required to enter the app. Email is processed by Supabase Auth. Cloud backups remain manual and optional in this beta. They are provided by Supabase. Cloud backups do not happen automatically.

Clearing website data or changing devices may delete this information. Export a backup using Backups & App Settings. Downloaded backups are unencrypted JSON; protect them because they may contain personal contact details, pet microchip numbers, and photos. If you choose to save a cloud backup, the same data is sent over HTTPS to a user-restricted Supabase table. These records are not end-to-end encrypted. You can sign out, but this does not delete local records. This is a beta and the policy needs completion before public launch.

If you choose to use the Google Photos integration after it is configured, Google's authorization and photo picker apply. The app should receive only photos you explicitly select. Google Photos access is not required to use the app.

Email updates: During account registration you may choose, via a separate unchecked box, to receive optional Pawbound travel tips, destination guides, and product updates. This choice is stored in a private Supabase table and can be changed later in My Account & Backups. If you do not opt in, creating an account does not authorize promotional emails. Essential account/security messages may still be sent. We have not enabled a bulk marketing mail service. Future promotional emails must include an unsubscribe mechanism.

Links to external travel rules and shopping websites are operated by their respective providers. Affiliate links, when introduced, will be identified.

Before public launch: Add the legal business name, privacy contact address, data retention policies, third-party services, and any server-side collection details. This draft is not a complete final privacy policy.
