Pawbound travel information · Beta

Pet travel laws, airline policies, fares, vaccination rules, quarantine rules, and approved entry points change and depend on route, species, origin, transit, date, and carrier.

Information in this beta is an organizer and starting point, not a guarantee of eligibility to travel. Verify every requirement with the government agencies, airlines, ferry, train, or bus operators responsible for your itinerary before booking or departure. Consult your veterinarian for individual health advice.

Before public launch: Complete legally appropriate terms, ownership and support details, and safety/privacy review.

A free account is required to use the beta app. Pawbound is intended for adults managing pet travel; keep your password private. Account creation does not require consent to promotional emails. Country requirements may change and should always be verified with official authorities.
